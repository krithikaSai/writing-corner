Privacy Policy

Writing Corner · Last updated 19 November 2026

Writing Corner is a free writing app. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your writing stays with you

Everything you write or create in the app (chapters, characters, places, notes, whiteboards and pictures you upload) is saved in your own browser on your own device. It is not sent to the app's author or to any server run by the app's author. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks for one permission only: access to its own private, hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). Your writing is backed up to that folder so you can open it on other devices. The app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive, and the app's author cannot see your data. The app does not collect your name, email address or any other profile information.

You can stop syncing at any time by removing Writing Corner's access in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party access).

Backups you download

If you download a backup file or choose a backup file on your device, it is saved wherever you choose. Keep it somewhere safe.

Third-party services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts and, if you use sign-in, Google's sign-in library. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address, as with any website. The app does not share your writing with them.

Deleting your data

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes what the app has saved on your device. Data backed up to Google Drive can be removed by revoking the app's access in your Google Account settings, which also lets you delete the app's stored data.

Contact
