# ✦ Writing Corner

Writing Corner is a calm, distraction-free writing app for authors. Draft your chapters in a clean editor, build characters with layered portraits, map out the places in your story, brainstorm on sticky notes and a whiteboard, and track your writing on a calendar with a monthly goal. Choose from several themes and ambient sounds to set the mood. Everything is saved in your browser, with options to back up to a file or sync with Google Drive.
